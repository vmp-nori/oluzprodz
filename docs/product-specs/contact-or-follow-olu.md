# Contact or follow Olu

## User

Potential client or employer

## Outcome

The visitor can draft an email inquiry or open Olu's verified social profiles.

## Acceptance criteria

- The Instagram control links to https://www.instagram.com/oluzprodz/
- External-link behavior is keyboard accessible and clearly labeled
- The form collects the visitor's name, reply email, project type, and message
- Submitting opens a pre-addressed email draft with the form content preserved
- Required fields use native, accessible browser validation

## Failure modes

- The link is incorrect
- The control is inaccessible
- The visitor's form content is lost during the email handoff
- The interface implies direct delivery when it only opens an email draft
