# Product sense

## Product

A desktop-first personal portfolio showcasing Olu's sports photography, short-form videography, and media experience.

## Users and problem

Users:

- Potential clients
- Potential employers

Problem: Visitors need a fast, memorable way to assess Olu's visual work, experience, and availability through his public social presence.

## Initial workflows

| Workflow | User | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explore Olu's portfolio | Potential client or employer | The visitor understands Olu's style, capabilities, and experience. |
| Contact or follow Olu | Potential client or employer | The visitor opens Olu's verified Instagram profile. |

## Success

- A desktop visitor can understand Olu's creative focus from the first viewport.
- A visitor can view representative photography and reels when those assets are supplied.
- A visitor can reach Olu through the verified Instagram profile.
- Unsupported narrow screens receive a clear desktop-view message instead of a broken experience.

## Non-goals

- Mobile website experience
- Self-service gallery or CMS
- Email contact form
- Payments, commerce, accounts, or bookings
- Production deployment in the initial scaffold

Humans steer priorities and acceptance; agents execute within the repository harness described by this knowledge base.
